About

Hi, I'm Mark

I'm a data analyst based in Kona, Hawaiʻi. I love the hard questions: the ones where the data is messy, the stakes are real, and nobody is quite sure where to start.

I work closely with the people who will use the answers, so the findings are clear, trusted and actually put to work. I also speak and lead trainings, most recently at the Kona Coast Leadership Summit.

I'm here to be your human layer for non-human intelligence, to help train you and your employees on which tools are available to you and how to use them.
